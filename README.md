# Fountain Park Portal — mock-up

Bilingual (Indonesian / English) clickable mock-up of an online management and resident service portal for the Fountain Park (Griya Pancoran) PPPSRS, Pancoran, South Jakarta.

All names, figures and data are samples, not real Fountain Park data. No back end.

Served at https://fountain-park-portal.jeremy-0d4.workers.dev
